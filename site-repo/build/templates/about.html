About this site

Map viewPlat view

This site publishes the burial records and lot map of {{SITE_TITLE}} in Shackleford, Saline County, Missouri. It is maintained by the cemetery board so that families can find a grave, and so that the board can see at a glance which lots are open.

What you can do here

  • The map shows every lot on the aerial photo. Search a surname or a lot number, tap a lot for its record, and use the locate button to see roughly where you are standing.
  • The plat is the schematic drawing of the eight blocks and the newer lettered field, the same shape as the paper maps in the board's binder.
  • Each lot has a stake-out sheet with its corner coordinates and dimensions, printable on one page.

Accuracy

Every position on this site is approximate. The lot outlines were computed from the column widths written on the paper section maps and anchored at one point, the northwest inside corner of the loop drive. Absolute position is good to roughly 10 to 20 feet; the spacing between lots follows the paper and is better than that. Do not use these shapes to locate a grave for a burial, mark a monument, or settle a boundary question. When the cemetery is surveyed, the surveyed corners replace this geometry and the site is rebuilt from them.

Where the records come from

  • List of Names sheet. The board's own name list, the primary source for Sections A, B and C.
  • Paper section maps. The numbered grids for Sections A through H and the newer lettered east field, transcribed in September 2026. Where the sheet and the paper disagreed, the sheet reading was kept and the paper reading noted.
  • Plots Sold ledger. Who bought which lot and when. The purchase details stay with the board and are not published here; the public site says only that a lot is taken.

Records live in a Google Sheet the board edits. The site is rebuilt from that sheet, so a correction entered there appears here within a day.

Report a correction

Names, dates and lot numbers were read off handwritten paper, and some are certainly wrong. If you can correct one, or you know of a burial that is missing, please write to the board with the lot number and what should be changed.

Privacy and tracking

This site carries no analytics, no cookies and no third-party trackers. The only outside requests are for the map tiles and the web fonts. Purchaser names, prices, payment status and the board's own notes are never published.
